La vera Pizza Napoletana

Few ingredients. Lots of heat. Real craft.

Just like in Naples: an airy crust, a thin, soft centre and ingredients with a story behind them.

DOUGH

Rested for 72 hours

Just flour, water, salt and yeast. Resting the dough for 72 hours makes the pizza light, airy and easy to digest.

OVEN

Approx. 485 °C

We bake in Gozney Arc XL ovens. In just 70 to 90 seconds the crust rises light and airy.

INGREDIENTS

With origins

San Marzano tomatoes by La Carmela from around Roccapiemonte, where grandma comes from. Fior di latte, fresh basil from our local greengrocer and olive oil from Tricarico near Matera, mum’s hometown.

SERVICE

Served fresh

Every pizza is topped, baked and served right in front of your guests.

Napoli nel cuore

Pizza comes from Naples. So do we.

Naples is the home of Pizza Napoletana and the home of our family.

This is where pizza was invented, and where it is still baked with devotion today: in seconds, at high heat, with simple, excellent ingredients. That is exactly how we bake it. Our roots reach from Campania to Basilicata, and many of our ingredients come from exactly these regions.

Our love for the city, its pizza and its football goes into every dough we bake. With us, a piece of Naples comes to your party.

Forza Napoli!

Chi sono · About me

Ciao, I’m Alessio.

Pizza runs in our family. My father Gennaro, known to everyone as Naro, has been performing at events with Naro’s Singing Pizza for years. With him, the show comes first: he sings, entertains and bakes real Pizza Napoletana at the same time.

Over the last ten years I have worked countless events at his side. From that experience came my own concept: Alessio's Pizza and More. Here the focus is entirely on the pizza, as a catering solution for your event. That is how we grew our family business.

And if you want both: we also appear together. I make sure every guest is full and happy, and my father brings the atmosphere with music and pizza.

Visit Naro’s Singing Pizza →

Just like my father, I am supported by the whole family. They always lend a hand, and we could not do it without them. So with us you are not just a customer, you are a little bit part of the famiglia.

Alessio

How it works

Four steps to your pizza event

  1. Send an enquiry

    Date, location and an approximate number of guests are enough to start.

  2. Receive a quote

    You get a personal quote tailored to your event.

  3. Plan the details

    The pizzas from our menu are included. We are happy to discuss special requests. We agree on date, time and schedule together.

  4. Enjoy

    We set up, bake live and clear away at the end. You sit back and relax.
