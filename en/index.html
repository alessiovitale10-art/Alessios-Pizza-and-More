La vera Pizza Napoletana

Few ingredients. Lots of heat. Real craft.

Just like in Naples: an airy crust, a thin, soft centre and ingredients with a story behind them.

DOUGH

Rested for 72 hours

Just flour, water, salt and yeast. Resting the dough for 72 hours makes the pizza light, airy and easy to digest.

OVEN

Approx. 485 °C

We bake in Gozney Arc XL ovens. In just 70 to 90 seconds the crust rises light and airy.

INGREDIENTS

With origins

San Marzano tomatoes by La Carmela from around Roccapiemonte, where grandma comes from. Fior di latte, fresh basil from our local greengrocer and olive oil from Tricarico near Matera, mum’s hometown.

SERVICE

Served fresh

Every pizza is topped, baked and served right in front of your guests.

Napoli nel cuore

Pizza comes from Naples. So do we.

Naples is the home of Pizza Napoletana and the home of our family.

This is where pizza was invented, and where it is still baked with devotion today: in seconds, at high heat, with simple, excellent ingredients. That is exactly how we bake it. Our roots reach from Campania to Basilicata, and many of our ingredients come from exactly these regions.

Our love for the city, its pizza and its football goes into every dough we bake. With us, a piece of Naples comes to your party.

Forza Napoli!

Chi sono · About me

Ciao, I’m Alessio.

Pizza runs in our family. My father Gennaro, known to everyone as Naro, has been performing at events with Naro’s Singing Pizza for years. With him, the show comes first: he sings, entertains and bakes real Pizza Napoletana at the same time.

Over the last ten years I have worked countless events at his side. From that experience came my own concept: Alessio's Pizza and More. Here the focus is entirely on the pizza, as a catering solution for your event. That is how we grew our family business.

And if you want both: we also appear together. I make sure every guest is full and happy, and my father brings the atmosphere with music and pizza.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The key answers about your pizza event. Can’t find your question? Just get in touch.

How early should I book?

As early as possible, ideally as soon as your date is set. Weekends in the wedding and summer season in particular book up quickly. Still, feel free to ask at short notice, sometimes it works out.

What happens if it rains?

Rain is no problem for us: we work under our tent or parasol and keep baking as normal. In a storm or thunderstorm we may have to pause briefly. And in severe weather where wind or lightning puts guests and team at risk, safety comes first. We then agree with you on site how to continue.

Are there vegetarian pizzas?

Yes. Our Marinara with tomato sauce, olive oil and basil is even vegan. The Margherita and the Bianca also contain no meat or sausage.

How many pizzas are there?

During your event we keep baking fresh pizzas while stocks last. We agree on the quantity with you beforehand based on your number of guests.

For how many people?

We are flexible: there is no minimum and no maximum number of guests. Whether a small garden party or a large corporate event, just ask us.

What do you need on site?

Just a 220-volt power socket and level ground. We bring ovens, counter, ingredients and equipment; otherwise we are completely self-sufficient.

Do you take care of set-up and clearing away?

Yes. We set up before the event, bake live and clear everything away at the end. You don’t have to worry about a thing.

Can I request my own pizzas?

Of course. The pizzas from our menu are included; custom pizzas are possible for an extra charge. Just ask us.

What about allergens?

Information on allergens and additives is available on request and on site. Ideally, let us know in your enquiry what we should look out for.

Which area do you cover?

Mainly in and around Mannheim. For events further away, just ask and we will find a solution.

Can I book pizza catering and Naro’s Singing Pizza together?

Yes, my father and I also appear together. I make sure all guests are well fed, and Naro brings the atmosphere with music and pizza.

How do I send an enquiry?

Simply use the form below: enter date, location and approximate number of guests and send it via WhatsApp or e-mail. You will receive a personal, no-obligation quote from us.
