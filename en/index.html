FAQ

Good questions.

How early should I book?

As early as possible, ideally as soon as your date is set. Weekends in the wedding and summer season in particular book up quickly. Still, feel free to ask at short notice, sometimes it works out.

What happens if it rains?

Rain is no problem for us: we work under our tent or parasol and keep baking as normal. In a storm or thunderstorm we may have to pause briefly. And in severe weather where wind or lightning puts guests and team at risk, safety comes first. We then agree with you on site how to continue.

Are there vegetarian pizzas?

Yes. Our Marinara with tomato sauce, olive oil and basil is even vegan. The Margherita and the Bianca also contain no meat or sausage.

How many pizzas are there?

During your event we keep baking fresh pizzas while stocks last. We agree on the quantity with you beforehand based on your number of guests.

For how many people?

We are flexible: there is no minimum and no maximum number of guests. Whether a small garden party or a large corporate event, just ask us.

What do you need on site?

Just a 220-volt power socket and level ground. We bring ovens, counter, ingredients and equipment; otherwise we are completely self-sufficient.

Do you take care of set-up and clearing away?

Yes. We set up before the event, bake live and clear everything away at the end. You don’t have to worry about a thing.

Can I request my own pizzas?

Of course. The pizzas from our menu are included; custom pizzas are possible for an extra charge. Just ask us.

What about allergens?

Information on allergens and additives is available on request and on site. Ideally, let us know in your enquiry what we should look out for.

Which area do you cover?

We are based in Mannheim, but we travel with no limits. Wherever your event is, just ask and we will find a solution.

Can I book pizza catering and Naro’s Singing Pizza together?

Yes, my father and I also appear together. I make sure all guests are well fed, and Naro brings the atmosphere with music and pizza.

How do I send an enquiry?

Simply use the form below: enter date, location and approximate number of guests and send it by e-mail. You will receive a personal, no-obligation quote from us.
